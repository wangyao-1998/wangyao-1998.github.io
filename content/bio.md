I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at [Nanyang Technological University, Singapore](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/), working with Associate Professor [Fei Xunchang](https://dr.ntu.edu.sg/entities/person/Fei-Xunchang) at the intersection of waste management, climate change, and One Health. I investigate solid waste biogeochemistry, pollutant release, and greenhouse gas emission mechanisms to inform sustainable waste management. I have published 18 papers in leading journals, including sole-first-author articles in *Nature Sustainability* and *One Earth*, and co-authored articles in *Nature Climate Change*, *Nature Reviews Materials*, and *Nature Reviews Earth & Environment*.

My research has been adopted by [Climate TRACE](https://climatetrace.org/), the world's largest independent greenhouse gas emissions inventory. I also contributed to the development of [WasteMAP](https://wastemap.earth/) with RMI, a global decision-support platform supporting waste methane assessment and mitigation planning in countries including the Philippines, Mexico, Brazil, and Nigeria. My findings also inform policy assessments by the OECD and the U.S. Department of Energy.

My sole-first-author *Nature Sustainability* paper was one of only 25 studies selected worldwide for the 2026 Frontiers Planet Prize: Singapore's only selected study and the only one focused on solid waste globally. I also received the Chinese Government Award for Outstanding Self-Financed Students Abroad, a highly selective China Scholarship Council honour with approximately 650 recipients worldwide.
